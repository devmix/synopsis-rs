# Performance Review Process

## Overview
LearnTech Solutions conducts performance reviews twice annually to support employee growth and align individual goals with company objectives.

## Review Cycle

### Mid-Year Review (June)
- Focus on progress assessment
- Goal adjustment if needed
- Development planning

### Year-End Review (December)
- Comprehensive performance evaluation
- Compensation review
- Promotion consideration

## Review Components

### 1. Self-Assessment
Employees complete a self-assessment covering:
- Achievement of goals
- Key accomplishments
- Challenges faced
- Learning and development

### 2. Manager Assessment
Managers evaluate:
- Performance against goals
- Core competencies
- Leadership behaviors
- Impact on team and company

### 3. Peer Feedback (Optional)
- 3-5 colleague feedback requests
- Anonymous submissions
- Focus on collaboration and impact

### 4. Calibration
- Department-level calibration meetings
- Ensures consistency across teams
- Review of rating distribution

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Description | Distribution Target |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Exceeds Expectations | Consistently delivers above role requirements | 15-20% |
| Meets Expectations | Fully meets role requirements | 65-75% |
| Needs Improvement | Partially meets requirements | 5-10% |
| Does Not Meet | Significantly below requirements | 0-5% |

## Competency Framework

### Technical Skills
- Code quality and best practices
- Technical problem solving
- Architecture and design
- Innovation and learning

### Collaboration
- Communication effectiveness
- Team support
- Knowledge sharing
- Cross-functional work

### Leadership
- Initiative and ownership
- Mentorship
- Decision making
- Strategic thinking

## Development Planning
Each review includes a development plan with:
- Skill gaps identification
- Learning resources
- Mentorship opportunities
- Stretch assignments
