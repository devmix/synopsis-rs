# HR Hiring Policy

## Overview
This document defines the hiring policy for LearnTech Solutions, an EdTech company specializing in adaptive learning platforms.

## Recruitment Process

### Step 1: Job Requisition
- Department head submits a hiring request
- HR reviews budget approval
- Position classification (Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead)

### Step 2: Candidate Sourcing
- Internal referrals (recommended)
- LinkedIn and job boards
- University partnerships for entry-level roles

### Step 3: Screening
- HR initial screening (30 minutes)
- Technical assessment for engineering roles
- Portfolio review for design roles

### Step 4: Interviews
- Technical interview (1 hour)
- Cultural fit interview (45 minutes)
- Final interview with department head (30 minutes)

### Step 5: Offer
- Salary negotiation based on level
- Benefits package explanation
- Start date coordination

## Compensation Levels

| Level | Salary Range (USD) | Experience |
|-------|-------------------|------------|
| Junior | $65,000 - $85,000 | 0-2 years |
| Mid | $85,000 - $110,000 | 2-5 years |
| Senior | $110,000 - $145,000 | 5-8 years |
| Lead | $145,000 - $180,000 | 8+ years |

## Benefits
- Health, dental, vision insurance
- 401(k) with 4% company match
- 20 days PTO + holidays
- Professional development budget ($2,000/year)
- Remote work flexibility

## Diversity & Inclusion
LearnTech Solutions is committed to building a diverse team. We encourage applications from underrepresented groups in tech.
