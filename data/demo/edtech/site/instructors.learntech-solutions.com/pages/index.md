## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to become an instructor?
There are no upfront fees or monthly charges. We only make money when you make money through our revenue share model.

### How do I get paid?
Payments are processed weekly via Stripe. You need a minimum balance of $50 to request a payout.

### Can I sell the same course on other platforms?
Yes, you retain full ownership of your content and can distribute it elsewhere.

### What tools do I need?
- Computer with internet connection
- Microphone for audio
- Camera (webcam or better)
- Screen recording software (we provide recommendations)

### How long does it take to create a course?
Most instructors complete their first course in 4-8 weeks, depending on content complexity.
